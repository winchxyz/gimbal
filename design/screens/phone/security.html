A NEAR contract owns every exchange account and asks the NEAR MPC network to sign, one order at a time, only after checking it against your policy.

How an order gets signed
  1. 1You sign the policyonce; again for every change
  2. 2The operator proposes an orderit holds no key
  3. 3The contract checks itasset, size, leverage, price, destination
  4. 4NEAR MPC signs it5 to 7 seconds
  5. 5The exchange fills itHyperliquid, Phoenix or Orderly

The operator cannot

  • Withdraw to any address but yours
  • Trade outside the policy
  • Change the policy or unpause
  • Get a key: there is none

Only you can

  • Pause the deskRescue still runs
  • Close both legs
  • Withdraw, always to demo-owner.near
  • Change the policy
