private keys exist for this desk

Not ours, not the bot’s, not yours. A NEAR contract owns every exchange account and asks the NEAR MPC network to sign, one order at a time, only after checking it against your policy.

How an order gets signed

  1. You sign the policydemo-owner.near, once; again for every change
  2. The operator proposes an orderthe desk engine; it holds no key
  3. The gimbal contract checks itasset, size, leverage, price against the oracle, destination
  4. NEAR MPC signs itv1.signer, in 5 to 7 seconds
  5. The exchange fills itHyperliquid, Phoenix or Orderly

If step 3 fails, nothing is signed and the refusal goes in the Journal.

Your policy

    Signed by demo-owner.near on 21 Oct · Change policy

    The operator can

    • Propose an orderThe contract builds it, checks it, then signs or refuses.
    • Propose a margin moveOnly between the desk’s own accounts, through a signed Intents quote, up to $500 an hour.
    • Refresh oracle pricesRead-only; costs gas, moves no money.

    The operator cannot

    • Withdraw to any address but yours
    • Trade an asset or size outside the policy
    • Change the policy or unpause the desk
    • Get a key: there is none to give

    Only you can

    • Pause the deskStops new orders at once. Rescue still runs.
    • Close both legs
    • WithdrawAlways to demo-owner.near.
    • Change the policyA new signature from your wallet each time.
    • Replace the operator
